# Elote — Oven Street Corn (×0.5, 2 ears)

Quantities: Tasting Table's 4-ear elote recipe, halved.
Method: broiler adaptation, because there is no grill. The broil time is an estimate. Watch the corn, not the clock.

- **Sauce:** Tue 5:55 PM
- **Broiler on:** 6:50 PM
- **Corn in:** 6:55 PM
- **Done:** about 7:08 PM

## Equipment

- Sheet pan lined with foil
- Tongs
- Small bowl + spoon or brush
- Oven with a broiler

## Ingredients

- 2 ears corn, shucked
- 1 Tbsp vegetable oil
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ¼ cup sour cream (or Mexican crema)
- 2 Tbsp mayonnaise
- Juice of ¼ lime
- ¼ cup crumbled cotija, plus extra on top
- Chipotle powder for dusting (optional)
- Lime wedges

## Prep checklist

- [ ] Shuck the corn and pull off the silk
- [ ] Crumble the cotija onto a plate
- [ ] Cut lime wedges

## Steps

1. 5:55 PM — Stir the sour cream, mayonnaise and lime juice together. Refrigerate.
2. 6:50 PM — Move an oven rack to about 6 inches below the broiler. Broiler on high.
3. Rub the corn with the oil, salt and pepper. Set on the foil-lined pan.
4. 6:55 PM — Broil about **10–12 minutes**, turning with tongs every **3 minutes**, until charred in spots all around.
5. Spread the sauce over the hot corn. Sprinkle with cotija, pressing it on. Dust with chipotle powder.
6. Tent with foil until dinner. Serve with lime wedges.

## How you know it's done

- Kernels are bright yellow with dark brown to black blisters on every side.

## If there is no fresh corn

- Use 1 bag frozen corn kernels (about 2 cups for two people).
- Thaw and pat dry. Sear in the hot cast iron with the oil, without stirring much, until browned in spots.
- Stir in the sauce and cotija off the heat. Serve in a bowl.
- Do this before the shrimp, then wipe out the skillet.
