# Elote — Street corn, oven style

Broiled corn with crema, cotija and chile.

- **Makes:** 4 ears
- **Source:** [Tasting Table, Elotes (quantities). Broiler method adapted: no grill.](https://www.tastingtable.com/cook/recipes/grilled-mexican-street-corn-recipe-elotes)
- **First step:** 8:18 AM · Your kitchen · morning
- **Last step:** 7:30 PM · Her kitchen

## Equipment

- Sheet pan lined with foil
- Tongs
- Oven mitt
- 2-cup tub with a lid
- Plate
- Oven with a broiler

## Ingredients

- **4 ears** corn
- **2 tablespoons** vegetable oil
- **1 teaspoon** salt
- **1 teaspoon** black pepper
- **½ cup** sour cream
- **¼ cup** mayonnaise
- **1 tablespoon** lime juice, the juice of half a lime
- **½ cup** crumbled cotija cheese, plus a little extra
- **Optional** chipotle powder, a light dusting
- **1** lime, cut in wedges for the table; an extra, not in the recipe

## Steps

Some steps say “tap” or mention the guide. That is the phone app. On paper, set your own timer for every **Timer** line.

### Your kitchen · morning

1. **8:18 AM — Zest half a lime, then juice eleven** (**18 min**; shared step, it also covers other dishes)
    - Rinse one lime. First the zest: scrape the green skin off half of it into the marinade jar. Green only, not the white underneath.
    - Now the juice. Roll each lime hard on the counter. Cut it in half across the middle.
    - Squeeze each half in the squeezer, cut side facing down, over a medium bowl.
    - Juice 11 limes. The zested lime is one of them. Pick out any seeds.
    - Measure ¾ cup of the juice into the ceviche lime jar. That is one ½ cup plus one ¼ cup. Lid on. Put a strip of tape or a rubber band on this jar so you can tell the two lime jars apart. Fridge.
    - Pour the rest (about ½ cup) into the sauce lime jar. Lid on. Fridge.
    - The slaw dressing, the corn sauce and the marinade each use some. About 1 tablespoon will be left over.
    - You need:
        - 11 limes (keep 4 whole)
        - Lime squeezer
        - Medium bowl
        - Measuring cup
        - Fine grater or vegetable peeler
        - Cutting board and knife
    - Goes into: Ceviche lime jar, sauce lime jar and marinade jar
    - Tip: Less than ½ cup left for the sauce lime jar? Juice 1 or 2 of the 4 whole limes too. Keep 2 whole for the wedges.
2. **8:46 AM — Stir the corn sauce** (**3 min**)
    - In one of the 2-cup tubs: ½ cup of sour cream, ¼ cup of mayonnaise and 1 tablespoon of lime juice from the sauce lime jar.
    - Stir until smooth. Lid on. Fridge.
    - You need:
        - Sour cream
        - Mayonnaise
        - Sauce lime jar
    - Goes into: Corn sauce tub

### Her kitchen

3. **4:03 PM — Husk the corn. Crumble the cheese.** (**6 min**)
    - Pull the green husks and the silky threads off all 4 ears.
    - Lay the corn on the foil-lined sheet pan.
    - Crumble cotija cheese into the 1-cup measure until it is half full. Tip it onto a plate. Crumble a little extra on top.
    - Cover the plate of cheese with foil. Fridge.
    - Goes into: Sheet pan and plate
4. **6:43 PM — Broiler on. Oil the corn.** (**3 min**)
    - Is the oven rack about 6 inches below the top? If not, move it now, before the oven is hot.
    - Then turn the oven broiler to high.
    - Turn on the fan over the stove.
    - Drizzle 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil over the 4 ears on the pan.
    - Sprinkle on 1 teaspoon of salt and 1 teaspoon of black pepper.
    - Roll the ears around so they are coated.
    - The broiler needs **a few minutes** to get hot. The guide tells you when the corn goes in.
    - You need:
        - Vegetable oil
        - Salt
        - Pepper
    - Not before 6:43 PM.
5. **6:50 PM — Corn under the broiler** (**1 min**)
    - Slide the pan under the broiler.
    - Tap Done as soon as it is in. A **12-minute** timer starts.
    - Every **3 minutes** the guide tells you to turn the corn.
    - To turn it: pull the rack part-way out with the oven mitt. Give each ear a quarter turn with tongs. Slide it back.
    - The time is an estimate. You want dark brown and black spots on every side.
    - You need:
        - Tongs
        - Oven mitt
    - Not before 6:50 PM.
    - **Timer: 12 minutes.** Corn broiling. Keep working. It ends about 7:03 PM.
    - At 6:54 PM, 6:57 PM and 7:00 PM: Turn the corn.
    - When it ends: Take the corn out and dress it.
    - **Warning:** Do not reach in under the broiler. Pull the rack out to you.
6. **7:03 PM — Take the corn out and dress it** (**4 min**)
    - Look first. Dark brown and black spots on every side? Then it is ready.
    - No dark spots yet? Leave it in. Turn it every **2 minutes**, for up to **6 more minutes**.
    - Turn the broiler off. Pull the rack part-way out with the oven mitt. Lift the pan out with both hands: the oven mitt on one, a thick folded dry towel on the other.
    - Set the pan on a free part of the stovetop or on a folded dry towel. Never on a bare counter.
    - The pan stays very hot. Do not touch it. Spoon the corn sauce over each ear and spread it with the back of the spoon.
    - Sprinkle the cotija all over. It sticks to the sauce. Do not press it on with your fingers.
    - Dust lightly with chipotle powder if you like.
    - Tent the pan loosely with foil to keep it warm.
    - You need:
        - Corn sauce tub
        - Plate of cotija
        - Foil
        - Oven mitt
        - Folded dry kitchen towel
    - Look for: Plump, shiny kernels with dark brown and black spots on every side. Your corn is white, so it will not turn yellow.
7. **7:26 PM — Last touches** (**2 min**; shared step, it also covers other dishes)
    - The beans must be steaming and bubbling. Not yet? Turn the burner up to medium. Stir until they are.
    - Take the small bowl of chopped cilantro out of the fridge. Stir it into the beans.
    - Pull the 2 serranos out of the rice.
    - Check that every burner and the oven are off.
    - Beans, rice, corn and shrimp to the table. Never put a hot pot or pan on the bare table. Put a folded dry towel or a wooden board under it.
    - Not before 7:25 PM.
8. **7:30 PM — Dinner is served** (shared step, it also covers other dishes)
    - Build each taco: shrimp, a pile of slaw, a drizzle of pink sauce, a squeeze of lime.
    - Scoop the ceviche with tostadas, chips or saltines.
    - Pickled onions go on everything.
    - Put leftover ceviche back in the fridge right after dinner. Throw it away if it sat out for more than **2 hours**.
    - All other leftovers go in the fridge within **2 hours**.

## If you did not soak the beans

### Your kitchen · morning

## Good to know

- The broiler time is an estimate. Watch the corn, not the clock.
- Done means plump, shiny kernels with dark brown and black spots on every side. White corn stays pale.
