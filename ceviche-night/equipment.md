## Churros: what it takes

Needed:
- Deep-fry/candy thermometer (essential)
- Churro press, or a sturdy piping bag with a large star tip (the dough is stiff; a thin disposable bag can burst)
- Heavy high-sided pot or Dutch oven
- Slotted spoon or spider

Where to get it by Tuesday afternoon:
- Target or Walmart (also on Instacart): thermometer, piping bag + large star tip set
- Sur La Table or a restaurant supply store: all of it, including a churro press
- A churro press is unlikely at a grocery store
