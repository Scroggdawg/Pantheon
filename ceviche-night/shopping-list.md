## How the limes add up

- Ceviche juice: ¾ cup = 6 limes
- Taco marinade: 2 Tbsp = 1 lime
- Slaw: ¼ cup = 2 limes
- Elote: ½ lime
- Zest lime: 1
- Wedges: 1
- Subtotal: about 11¼, plus 25% = 14 → buy 15
