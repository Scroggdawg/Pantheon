# The Blanch — Pickled Red Onions (×1, about 2 cups)

Source: Mexico in My Kitchen.

- **Start:** Tue 7:45 AM (your kitchen)
- **Hands-on:** 15 minutes
- **Ready:** 10:00 AM or later (2 hours chilling)

## Equipment

- Kettle or pot (boiling water)
- Small dry skillet
- Colander
- Glass jar with a lid, 1 pint or larger

## Ingredients

- 2 medium red onions, thinly sliced
- 4–5 cups water (blanching)
- ½ cup white vinegar
- ½ cup water (brine)
- 1 tsp dried Mexican oregano
- 2 whole allspice berries
- 3 black peppercorns
- 1 bay leaf
- About ½ tsp salt

## Prep checklist

- [ ] Slice the onions into thin rings or half-moons
- [ ] Measure vinegar and brine water
- [ ] Count out the spices

## Steps

1. Toast the oregano in the dry skillet over medium-high about **30 seconds**, until fragrant. Tip it out right away so it doesn't burn.
2. Bring the blanching water to a boil. Add the onions and turn off the heat. Wait **2 minutes**. Drain well.
   - You like strong onion flavor: cut this to **10–30 seconds**. They still turn pink.
3. In the jar, combine onions, vinegar, brine water, oregano, allspice, peppercorns, bay leaf and salt. Stir or shake.
4. Cover and refrigerate about **2 hours**.

## How you know it's done

- Onions are bright pink and bend without snapping.

## Storage and make-ahead

- Keeps up to 2 weeks in the fridge in the brine.
- Use a clean fork to take them out.
