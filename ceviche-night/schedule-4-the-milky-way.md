# The Milky Way — Red tomato rice

Rice toasted until milky white, then cooked in tomato and broth.

- **Makes:** About 3 cups
- **Source:** [Rick Bayless, Red Tomato Rice](https://www.rickbayless.com/recipe/red-tomato-rice)
- **First step:** 8:57 AM · Your kitchen · morning
- **Last step:** 7:26 PM · Her kitchen

## Equipment

- Blender
- Saucepan with a tight lid that holds 6 to 8 cups
- Wooden spoon
- Fork
- Purée jar, broth jar, zip bag

## Ingredients

- **Half a can** fire-roasted diced tomatoes, lightly drained, about ¾ cup
- **¼ small** white onion, a chunk the size of a ping-pong ball
- **1** garlic clove, cut in half
- **2¼ teaspoons** vegetable oil
- **¾ cup** white rice, do not rinse it
- **¾ cup + 2 tablespoons** chicken broth
- **2** serrano chiles, whole, with a slit down one side
- **2 tablespoons** chopped parsley
- **A generous ¼ teaspoon** salt, ¾ teaspoon if the broth is unsalted
- **Optional** a handful of shredded carrots

## Steps

Some steps say “Tap Done” or mention the guide. That is the phone app. On paper, set your own timer for every **Timer** line.

### Your kitchen · morning

1. **8:57 AM — Blend the rice purée** (**6 min**)
    - Open the fire-roasted tomatoes. Spoon half the can into the blender. Leave most of the watery juice behind.
    - Add a chunk of white onion about the size of a ping-pong ball, cut in 3 or 4 pieces. Cut it from one of your wedges.
    - Add 1 peeled garlic clove, cut in half.
    - Blend until completely smooth.
    - Pour it into the purée jar. Use one of the 2-cup jars. Lid on. Fridge. Rinse the blender right away.
    - You need:
        - Fire-roasted tomatoes
        - White onion wedge
        - Garlic
        - Blender
    - Goes into: Purée jar
    - Tip: Blender just spinning? Stop it first. Push the tomatoes down with a spoon. Add 1 tablespoon of the juice from the can. Blend again.
2. **9:03 AM — Pack the rice kit** (**3 min**)
    - Measure ¾ cup of white rice into the zip bag. Do not rinse it.
    - Measure ¾ cup plus 2 tablespoons of chicken broth into the broth jar. Use one of the 2-cup jars.
    - Add ¼ teaspoon of salt plus a pinch to that jar. Use ¾ teaspoon if your broth says unsalted.
    - Lid on. Broth jar in the fridge. Rice bag by the door.
    - You need:
        - White rice
        - Chicken broth
        - Salt
        - Measuring cup
    - Goes into: Zip bag and broth jar

### Her kitchen

3. **3:40 PM — Get the rice add-ins ready** (**3 min**)
    - Take 2 serranos. Leave them whole, but cut a slit down one side of each.
    - Chop parsley leaves until you have 2 tablespoons.
    - Both go in one small bowl.
    - Goes into: Small bowl (rice add-ins)
4. **6:22 PM — Line up everything for the rice** (**2 min**)
    - Put these next to the stove. Once the rice starts you cannot leave the pan for **10 minutes**.
    - [ ] Saucepan and its lid
    - [ ] Wooden spoon and a fork
    - [ ] Vegetable oil and measuring spoons
    - [ ] Rice bag
    - [ ] Purée jar, from the fridge
    - [ ] Broth jar, from the fridge
    - [ ] Bowl of serranos and parsley
    - Not before 6:22 PM.
5. **6:25 PM — Toast the rice** (**7 min**)
    - Saucepan on the smallest burner, on medium. Add 2¼ teaspoons of vegetable oil. Let it heat for **1 minute**.
    - Pour in the rice from the bag. Start the timer.
    - Stir almost the whole time.
    - You need:
        - Saucepan
        - Vegetable oil
        - Rice bag
        - Wooden spoon
    - Not before 6:25 PM.
    - **Timer: 5 minutes 30 seconds.** Rice toasting. Stay here.
    - Look for: The grains turn from see-through to chalky white.
    - Tip: Not chalky white when the timer ends? Keep stirring for up to **1 more minute**.
6. **6:32 PM — Add the tomato purée** (**3 min**)
    - Pour in the purée jar. It will spit, so stand back.
    - Start the timer. Keep stirring.
    - You need:
        - Purée jar
    - **Timer: 2 minutes 30 seconds.** Purée cooking down. Stay here.
    - Look for: Thick and fairly dry, like wet sand.
7. **6:35 PM — Add the broth and bring it to a boil** (**2 min**)
    - Pour in the broth jar. Burner up to high. It boils in about **1 minute**. Stay right there.
    - When it reaches a full boil, stir in the serranos and parsley from the small bowl.
    - Scrape down any rice stuck to the sides of the pan.
    - Go straight to the next step.
    - You need:
        - Broth jar
        - Bowl of serranos and parsley
        - Lid
    - Tip: Optional: stir in a handful of shredded carrots too.
8. **6:37 PM — Lid on. Lowest heat.** (**1 min**)
    - Put the lid on tight.
    - Turn the burner to its lowest setting.
    - Electric or glass stove? It stays hot. Move the pan to another burner set to its lowest setting.
    - Tap Done. A **20-minute** timer starts.
    - Do not lift the lid until it ends.
    - **Timer: 20 minutes.** Rice cooking. Keep working. It ends about 6:58 PM.
    - When it ends: Fluff the rice.
    - Tip: Lid loose? Lay a sheet of foil over the pan and press the lid down on top.
9. **6:58 PM — Fluff the rice** (**1 min**)
    - Burner off.
    - Lift the lid. Drag a fork through the rice to separate the grains.
    - Lid back on. Leave it.
    - Stir the beans while you are at the stove. Bubbling all over? Turn them down to low.
    - Look for: No liquid left. The grains are tender.
    - Tip: Still wet at the bottom, or hard when you bite a grain? Lid back on. Lowest heat. **5 more minutes**. Pan dry and grains still hard? Sprinkle on a splash of water first.
10. **7:26 PM — Last touches** (**2 min**; shared step, it also covers other dishes)
    - The beans must be steaming and bubbling. Not yet? Turn the burner up to medium. Stir until they are.
    - Stir the bowl of chopped cilantro into the beans.
    - Pull the 2 serranos out of the rice.
    - Check that every burner and the oven are off.
    - Beans, rice, corn and shrimp to the table.
    - Not before 7:25 PM.

## Good to know

- Do not lift the lid during the **20 minutes**.
- Covered and off the heat, the rice stays hot for about **30 minutes**.
- To reheat: add a splash of broth, cover, warm gently.
