# The Milky Way — Red Tomato Rice (×0.5)

Source: Rick Bayless, Red Tomato Rice.

- **Purée:** Mon 9:50 AM (your kitchen, so the blender stays home)
- **Start:** 6:25 PM (her kitchen)
- **Off heat:** 7:02 PM
- **Holds covered until:** 7:30 PM

## Equipment

- Blender
- Small saucepan (1½–2 qt) with a tight lid. A half batch is shallow in a 3-qt pot, so use a smaller one if she has it.
- Wooden spoon
- Fork
- Jar for the purée

## Ingredients

- Half of a 15-oz can fire-roasted diced tomatoes, lightly drained (about ¾ cup)
- ¼ small white onion, chopped
- 1 garlic clove, halved
- 2¼ tsp vegetable oil
- ¾ cup white rice (don't rinse)
- ⅞ cup chicken broth (¾ cup + 2 Tbsp)
- 2 serranos, slit lengthwise
- 2 Tbsp chopped parsley
- A generous ¼ tsp salt (¾ tsp if the broth is unsalted)
- Optional: a handful of shredded carrot

## Prep checklist

- [ ] Purée made and jarred
- [ ] Rice measured
- [ ] Broth measured
- [ ] Serranos slit down one side (leave whole)
- [ ] Parsley chopped

## Steps

1. 9:50 AM — Blend the tomatoes, onion and garlic until smooth. Jar and refrigerate.
2. 6:25 PM — Heat the oil in the saucepan over medium. Add the rice and stir often **5–6 minutes**, until the grains turn from see-through to milky white.
3. Add the purée. Cook **2–3 minutes**, stirring, until reduced and fairly dry.
4. Add the broth and bring to a full boil. Stir in the serranos, parsley, salt and carrot. Scrape down the sides.
5. 6:42 PM — Cover. Cook on the lowest heat **20 minutes**. Don't lift the lid.
6. 7:02 PM — Fluff with a fork, re-cover, and let stand off the heat.
7. 7:26 PM — Remove the serranos. Serve.

## How you know it's done

- Step 2: grains look chalky white, not translucent.
- Step 6: all liquid absorbed, grains tender.

## Storage and make-ahead

- Covered off the heat it stays hot for about 30 minutes.
- To reheat: a splash of broth, cover, warm gently.
