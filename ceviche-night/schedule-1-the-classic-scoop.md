# The Classic Scoop — Classic Ceviche (×0.5)

Source: Rick Bayless, Classic Ceviche.

- **Lime juice:** Mon 9:30 AM (your kitchen)
- **Fish arrives:** Sun night with the Lazy Acres order. Coldest part of the fridge.
- **Cure:** 4:10 PM (her kitchen)
- **Cure done:** 4:35 PM
- **Mix:** 5:30 PM (her kitchen)
- **Serve:** 7:30 PM (or 6:00 as a starter)

## Safety — raw fish

- Lime juice does **not** kill parasites or bacteria.
- Use only **sushi-grade or previously frozen** fish from a fish counter you trust.
- It is delivered the night before. Keep it in the coldest part of the fridge, and on ice packs in the cooler for the drive.
- When it arrives, smell it. It should smell like clean seawater, not fishy or sour. If it smells off, don't make ceviche with it.
- Keep the ceviche cold until the moment it goes out. Throw away leftovers that sat out over 2 hours.

## Equipment

- Glass or stainless bowl (not aluminum; lime juice reacts with it)
- Sharp knife and board
- Lime squeezer
- Strainer or colander
- Cooler with ice packs

## Ingredients

- ½ lb skinless firm white fish, ½-inch cubes
- ¾ cup fresh lime juice (about 6 limes)
- ½ medium white onion, ¼-inch dice
- 2 medium ripe tomatoes (½ lb), ¼-inch dice
- 1 serrano, seeded and finely chopped
- 2 Tbsp + 2 tsp chopped cilantro, plus leaves for garnish
- 2 Tbsp + 2 tsp chopped pitted green olives
- 2¼ tsp extra-virgin olive oil (optional)
- A generous ¼ tsp salt
- 1½ Tbsp fresh orange juice
- ½ large ripe avocado, diced
- Tostadas, tortilla chips, saltines

## Prep checklist

At 9:30 AM:
- [ ] Juice the limes (¾ cup for this dish). Jar and refrigerate.

At 4:10 PM:
- [ ] Dice ½ white onion
- [ ] Cut fish into ½-inch cubes

At 4:20 PM:
- [ ] Dice tomatoes
- [ ] Seed and chop serrano (wash hands after; don't touch your eyes)
- [ ] Chop cilantro and olives
- [ ] Juice the orange

## Steps

1. 4:10 PM — In the glass bowl, combine fish, lime juice and onion. The fish should float freely. Cover. Refrigerate **20 minutes**.
2. 4:35 PM — Break a cube open. If it is opaque all the way through, it is done. If the center is still glassy, give it **5 more minutes**.
3. Drain and discard the lime juice. Put the fish and onion back in the fridge, covered.
4. 5:30 PM — In a large bowl, mix tomatoes, serrano, cilantro, olives and olive oil. Add the drained fish.
5. Season with the salt and orange juice. Taste on a chip. Cover and refrigerate.
6. 7:26 PM — Dice the avocado and gently fold it in. Garnish with cilantro leaves. Serve with tostadas, chips and saltines.

## How you know it's done

- Cured fish is white and opaque through the center, like cooked fish, but still tender.

## Storage and make-ahead

- Never leave the fish in the juice more than about 4 hours; it turns mushy.
- Add the tomato mix no more than 2 hours before serving.
- Avocado goes in at the last minute.
