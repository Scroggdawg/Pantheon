# The Street Cart — Churros con Cajeta (×0.5, 8 churros)

Source: Pati Jinich.

**Status: DROPPED for now (Luke, Mon Nov 23).** Not on the master schedule or the shopping list. Kept here in case it comes back. It needs a thermometer and a press or star-tip bag you don't own yet. See `equipment.md`.

- **Start:** after dinner
- **Total:** about 40 minutes (15 heating oil, 25 cooking)

## Hot-oil safety

- Never leave the oil unattended.
- Keep the pot handle turned inward.
- Keep a lid nearby. If the oil catches fire: lid on, heat off, **never use water**.
- Let the oil cool completely before moving or disposing of it.

## Equipment

- Heavy high-sided pot or Dutch oven
- Deep-fry/candy thermometer
- Churro press, or sturdy piping bag with a large star tip
- Medium saucepan + wooden spoon (dough)
- Kitchen scissors
- Slotted spoon or spider, tongs
- Sheet pan with a wire rack or paper towels
- Large plate (coating)
- Lid that fits the frying pot

## Ingredients

- Dough:
  - 1 cup water
  - 2 Tbsp vegetable oil
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - 2 Tbsp sugar
  - ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
  - ½ tsp kosher salt
  - 1 cup all-purpose flour
- Coating:
  - ½ cup sugar
  - 1½ tsp ground cinnamon
- About 6 cups vegetable oil (1½ inches deep in the pot)
- About ½ cup cajeta

## Prep checklist (do at 4:20 PM with the rest)

- [ ] Mix the coating sugar and cinnamon on a large plate
- [ ] Measure the flour into a bowl
- [ ] Measure dough sugar, cinnamon and salt into one small bowl
- [ ] Set out rack, scissors, spider, lid

## Steps

1. Heat the frying oil over medium to **350°F**, checked with the thermometer. Start this first. It takes 10–15 minutes.
2. Have the coating plate next to the stove.
3. In the saucepan, bring the water, 2 Tbsp oil, vanilla, sugar, cinnamon and salt to a boil over medium-high.
4. Dump in all the flour, turn off the heat, and stir hard about **2 minutes** until smooth. Cool slightly, until you can handle it.
5. Load the press or bag. Pipe 6–8 inch lengths into the oil and snip them off with scissors. Fry 3–4 at a time. Fry **3–4 minutes**, turning, until golden and crisp. Bring the oil back to 350°F between batches.
6. Drain briefly on the rack. Roll in the cinnamon sugar while very hot.
7. Warm the cajeta (microwave 20–30 seconds, stir). Serve.

## How you know it's done

- Deep golden, firm and crisp outside. Break one open: no raw, pasty center.

## Storage and make-ahead

- Don't refrigerate the dough; it hardens.
- Fry right before serving.
