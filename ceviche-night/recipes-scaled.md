# Scaled Recipes — 2 people

Multipliers (people ÷ base, rounded up to the nearest 0.5):

- The Classic Scoop: ×0.5
- Smoke & Pink: ×0.5 (6 tacos)
- The Patrón: ×0.5
- The Milky Way: ×0.5
- The Blanch: ×1
- The Smoky Cream: ×1
- The Street Cart: ×0.5 (8 churros) — undecided
- Elote: ×0.5 (2 ears)

Steps and timers live in each `schedule-*.md` file.

## 1. The Classic Scoop — ×0.5

- ½ lb skinless firm white fish, sushi-grade or previously frozen, ½-inch cubes
- ¾ cup fresh lime juice (about 6 limes)
- ½ medium white onion, ¼-inch dice
- ½ lb ripe tomatoes (2 medium), ¼-inch dice
- 1 serrano, seeded and finely chopped
- 2 Tbsp + 2 tsp chopped cilantro, plus leaves for garnish
- 2 Tbsp + 2 tsp chopped pitted green olives
- 2¼ tsp extra-virgin olive oil (optional)
- A generous ¼ tsp salt (⅜ tsp)
- 1½ Tbsp fresh orange juice
- ½ large ripe avocado, diced
- Tostadas, tortilla chips, saltines

## 2. Smoke & Pink — ×0.5

- ½ lb medium shrimp, peeled and deveined
- Marinade:
  - Zest of ½ lime
  - 2 Tbsp fresh lime juice
  - 1½ tsp chipotle powder (1 tsp for mild)
  - 1 Tbsp finely chopped cilantro
  - 1 garlic clove, minced
  - 2 Tbsp olive oil
  - ¼ tsp salt
  - ¼ tsp black pepper
- Pink sauce:
  - 6 Tbsp sour cream
  - 1–1½ Tbsp sriracha, to taste
- To cook and serve:
  - 1½ tsp olive oil
  - 6 small corn tortillas
  - 1 lime, in wedges
  - The Smoky Cream slaw

## 3. The Patrón — ×0.5

- ½ lb dried pinto beans (about 1¼ cups)
- 1 quart water for soaking
- 3½ cups fresh water + ½ cup beer for cooking
- 2 oz fatty pork shoulder, ½-inch cubes
- 4 thick slices bacon, ½-inch pieces
- ½ medium white onion, diced
- 1 large poblano, roasted, peeled, seeded, chopped
- Half of a 15-oz can tomatoes, drained and chopped
- ½ tsp salt, or to taste
- ¼ cup cilantro, roughly chopped

## 4. The Milky Way — ×0.5

- Half of a 15-oz can fire-roasted diced tomatoes, lightly drained (about ¾ cup)
- ¼ small white onion, chopped
- 1 garlic clove, halved
- 2¼ tsp vegetable oil
- ¾ cup white rice (don't rinse)
- ⅞ cup chicken broth (¾ cup + 2 Tbsp)
- 2 serranos, slit lengthwise
- 2 Tbsp chopped parsley
- A generous ¼ tsp salt (¾ tsp if the broth is unsalted)
- Optional: a handful of shredded carrot (in with the broth)

## 5. The Blanch — ×1

- 2 medium red onions, thinly sliced
- 4–5 cups water (blanching)
- ½ cup white vinegar
- ½ cup water (brine)
- 1 tsp dried Mexican oregano
- 2 whole allspice berries
- 3 black peppercorns
- 1 bay leaf
- About ½ tsp salt

## 6. The Smoky Cream — ×1

- 4 cups shredded green cabbage
- 1 cup shredded purple cabbage
- 2 medium carrots' worth of shredded carrot (about 1 cup from the bag)
- 1 small red onion, very thinly sliced
- 3 large scallions, thinly sliced
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced
- Dressing:
  - ½ cup mayonnaise
  - ½ cup full-fat plain Greek yogurt
  - ¼ cup fresh lime juice
  - 2 Tbsp sugar
  - 1 tsp ground cumin
  - 1 tsp chipotle powder
- About ½ tsp salt and ¼ tsp black pepper

This is the full batch. It covers 6 tacos with plenty left as a side.

## 7. The Street Cart — ×0.5 (if churros stay)

- Dough:
  - 1 cup water
  - 2 Tbsp vegetable oil
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - 2 Tbsp sugar
  - ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
  - ½ tsp kosher salt
  - 1 cup all-purpose flour
- Coating:
  - ½ cup sugar
  - 1½ tsp ground cinnamon
- About 6 cups vegetable oil for frying (not scaled; depth stays 1½ inches)
- About ½ cup cajeta

## 8. Elote — ×0.5

Quantities are from Tasting Table's 4-ear elote recipe, halved. The broiler method is an adaptation because there is no grill; the broil time is an estimate to watch, not a tested number.

- 2 ears corn, shucked
- 1 Tbsp vegetable oil
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ¼ cup sour cream (or Mexican crema)
- 2 Tbsp mayonnaise
- Juice of ¼ lime
- ¼ cup crumbled cotija, plus extra on top
- Chipotle powder for dusting (optional)
- Lime wedges
